PlayStation Cafe Management System

A lightweight desktop application for managing PlayStation devices in a cafe. Track sessions, handle billing, and generate reports, all offline, all local.

Download for Windows Download for Linux

Features

Dashboard

View all devices with real-time status, elapsed time, and estimated cost at a glance.

Session Management

Start timed (fixed duration) or open-ended sessions with a single click.

Automatic Billing

Minute-based billing in MKD with transparent pricing shown on session end.

Sound Alerts

Get notified when timed sessions expire. Mute toggle available for quiet hours.

Reports

View revenue for Today, Week, Month, or custom date ranges, filter by device.

Device Management

Add, edit, or remove devices with custom hourly rates. Auto-named PS1, PS2, etc.
